# Reporting-boundary checks

Primary issuer pages reviewed during the build on 2026-09-04. These are identity and coverage notes; financial evidence in generated briefings comes from archived documents, not search snippets. Current SEC identity/status checks are separately recorded in SOURCES.md and collection metadata.

* **Rocket / Mr. Cooper:** Rocket's [completed-acquisition announcement](https://www.rocketcompanies.com/press-release/rocket-companies-closes-14-2-billion-acquisition-of-mr-cooper/) states that the transaction closed on October 1, 2025 and that Mr. Cooper's servicing functions would be rebranded under Rocket. COOP is not an additional enabled peer. Comparisons across this acquisition boundary require matched populations; the tool does not assume continuity.
* **Onity:** The [official shareholder site](https://shareholders.onitygroup.com/) identifies Onity Group Inc. (NYSE: ONIT) and its primary operating subsidiary as Onity Mortgage Corporation. Older links still use PHH and Ocwen names. These brands must not become separate peers. The site also lists a July 2026 reverse-mortgage asset transaction, so servicing populations require current-document review.
* **UWM / Two Harbors:** UWM Holdings Corporation is the publicly traded indirect parent of United Wholesale Mortgage. A December 2025 proposed acquisition of Two Harbors is insufficient evidence of completion: the issuer's [June 4, 2026 update](https://investors.uwm.com/news/financial-news/news-details/2026/UWMC-Reaffirms-Commitment-to-Premium-Proposal-to-Acquire-Two-Harbors-for-12-50-Per-Share-in-Cash-with-Stock-Election/default.aspx) still discusses a proposal and a competing transaction. Treat the final reporting boundary as unresolved unless a later authoritative completion disclosure is obtained. The [official investor page](https://investors.uwm.com/) displayed Q2 results alongside a Q1 presentation during review; this illustrates why page recency cannot determine a deck's reporting period.
* **loanDepot:** The [official events page](https://investors.loandepot.com/events-and-presentations/) lists current earnings presentations and issuer-published transcript links. Its [August 21, 2026 listing-price notice](https://investors.loandepot.com/news/corporate-and-financial-news/corporate-and-financial-news-details/2026/loanDepot-Inc--Addresses-Notice-of-NYSE-Trading-Price-Non-Compliance/default.aspx) explicitly says there is no immediate impact on trading, business operations or SEC reporting requirements. A deficiency notice must not be converted into an unsupported inactive/delisted classification.
* **Rithm:** The [official investor page](https://ir.rithmcap.com/overview/default.aspx) describes a diversified asset manager and links a current quarterly release, presentation and filing. Consolidated Rithm assets or AUM are not mortgage-servicing assets; use the Newrez/servicing business definition in the underlying documents.

Candidates remain disabled until the user chooses to expand coverage and reviews the recorded verification limits. There is no implied ranking, comprehensive market universe, or guarantee of equally detailed servicing disclosure across companies.
